## 1️⃣ 💰 ESI, FINANCE & BUSINESS NEWS

📰 **India's Economy — Size, Growth & the Feb 1 Budget** *(merged: Jan's pre-Budget context + Feb's actual Budget content)*

🪝 Hook — India overtook Japan to become the world's 4th-largest economy in January, and a month later Nirmala Sitharaman used her record 9th-consecutive Budget to lock in a falling fiscal deficit and a ₹1,000 crore semiconductor push.

- India surpassed Japan: now **4th-largest economy at $4.18 trillion**; 2030 target — overtake Germany for 3rd place ($7.3 trillion projected)
- Real GDP grew **8.2%** in Q2 FY26 (from 7.8% Q1, 7.4% Q4 FY25); 1st Advance Estimates: FY26 real GDP growth **7.4%** (₹201.90 lakh Cr)
- **Economic Survey 2025-26** (tabled Jan 29 by Sitharaman, authored by CEA V. Anantha Nageswaran): projects FY27 real GDP growth at **6.8-7.2%** (down from FY26's 7.4%); Gross NPA 2.2%, Net NPA 0.5% (Sep 2025); fiscal deficit to decline to 4.4% of GDP in FY26 (from 9.2% in FY21)
- Growth forecasts (NSO figure retained as primary; others compressed): World Bank 6.5%, IMF 6.6%/6.2%, Moody's 6.4%/6.5%
  - 🚫 Also projected by OECD, S&P, ADB, Fitch — similar range, not repeating individually
- **Union Budget 2026-27** (presented Feb 1, 2026) — Sitharaman's **9th consecutive Budget**, a new record (surpassing her own prior streak); Morarji Desai still holds most Budgets overall (10, non-consecutive)
  - 1st Budget prepared in Kartavya Bhawan; built around 3 'Kartavyas' — growth, citizen aspirations, inclusive development
  - **Fiscal deficit** FY27 target **4.3% of GDP** (from 4.4% RE FY26) — continuing the consolidation path flagged in the Economic Survey
  - **Debt-to-GDP** – 55.6% (BE 2026-27) vs 56.1% (RE 2025-26) | **Capex** up 9% to record **₹12.2 lakh Cr (4.4% of GDP)**
  - **New Income Tax Act, 2025** effective **April 1, 2026** — sections cut 819→536
  - STT hiked: Futures 0.05% (from 0.02%), Options 0.15%; buyback tax 22%/30% (corporate/non-corporate promoters)
  - **'India Semiconductor Mission 2.0'** — ₹1,000 Cr outlay FY27 (ISM 1.0, Dec 2021, ₹76,000 Cr, has approved 10 projects worth ₹1.60 lakh Cr across 6 states)
  - **'Biopharma SHAKTI'** – ₹10,000 Cr | **SME Growth Fund** – ₹10,000 Cr | 7 new High-Speed Rail corridors (₹16 lakh Cr)
  - **16th Finance Commission** (Chair: Arvind Panagariya) recommendation accepted: vertical devolution retained at **41%** (2026-27 to 2030-31); new 10% weightage for 'Contribution to GDP' replaces 'Tax & Fiscal Effort' (2.5%); total grants ₹9.47 lakh Cr
  - **Gender Budget**: share up to **9.37%** (from 8.86%); GBS allocation ₹5.01 lakh Cr (+11.55%)

🧠 Mnemonic → "9th, 4.3, 4.4" — 9th consecutive Budget, 4.3% fiscal deficit target, 4.4% capex-to-GDP.

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — Two separate "most Budgets" records must not be conflated: Morarji Desai = most Budgets overall (10), Sitharaman = most **consecutive** (9, and counting). Also: "4th-largest economy, $4.18 trillion" is near-certain; don't confuse with the 2030 target of 3rd place.

💼 Interview Q → *"How does the FY27 Budget's fiscal path align with the Economic Survey's own projections?"* — The Economic Survey (Jan 29) projected FY27 growth moderating to 6.8-7.2% from FY26's 7.4%, and the Budget (Feb 1) responded with a tighter fiscal deficit target of 4.3% (from 4.4%) and a 9% capex hike — both documents show the government leaning on public investment to offset a expected growth slowdown rather than fiscal loosening.

## 2️⃣ 🏛️ REGULATORY BODIES NEWS (RBI / SEBI / IRDAI)

📰 **RBI — January-February 2026, consolidated regulatory sweep** *(merged: same regulator across both months, grouped by theme rather than by month)*

🪝 Hook — Across two months, RBI held the repo rate steady while quietly reshaping bank dividends, MSME lending, deposit insurance, acquisition financing, and even rolling out its first-ever 90-day repo — a genuinely dense two-month regulatory sweep from one institution.

**A) Monetary Policy**
- Repo rate held at **5.25%**, neutral stance, through both January (no separate MPC that month) and the **February MPC review**; confirmed still 5.25% as late as August 2026 (4th straight hold; next meeting Oct 5-8, 2026)
- Feb MPC: FY26 GDP growth raised to 7.4%; FY26 CPI ~2.1%; new online-fraud compensation framework (victim+bank 15% each, RBI covers rest, capped ₹25,000/case, from the Depositor Education & Awareness Fund's ₹85,000 Cr unclaimed-deposits pool)

**B) Liquidity Operations (January)**
- RBI's **first-ever 90-day Variable Rate Repo (VRR)** — ₹25,000 Cr on Jan 30, 2026 (previous longest: 56 days)
- Part of a ~₹2.15 lakh Cr liquidity package (Jan 30-Feb 12): also a $10 billion USD/INR Buy-Sell Swap (3-yr tenor) + ₹1 lakh Cr OMO purchases

**C) Bank Dividend Payout Cap** 🔄 *(UPDATE — finalised after both source PDFs were compiled)*
- Proposed in January: raising cap from 40% to 75% of PAT with a graded 10-bucket CET1-linked structure
- **Now finalised** (per Jan bucket's own status check, dated March 10, 2026): **75%** for commercial/small finance/payments banks, **80%** for RRBs/LABs — a split the original draft didn't have; effective FY27

**D) Prudential & Governance Norms (January cluster)**
- NBFC Prudential Norms Amendment: risk-weight relief for infra loans tied to repayment progress; effective Apr 1, 2026
- Credit Risk (Related Party Transactions): widened "related party" definition, size-tiered materiality thresholds; effective Apr 1, 2026
- PSL Targets correction: SFB target fixed at **60% of ANBC** (was mistakenly notified as 75%)
- UCB/Cooperative Bank director cooling-off: proposed 3-year gap after 10 years' continuous board service

**E) Kisan Credit Card (KCC) — revised draft norms (February)**
- Tenure extended to **6 years** (from 5); collateral/margin waived up to ₹2 lakh/borrower
- Crop tenures standardised (12 months short-duration, 18 months long); new flexible ₹10,000-50,000 limit for marginal farmers
- Feedback window closed March 6, 2026 — final notification status not verified

**F) Deposit Insurance — risk-based premium, DICGC (February)**
- Moves from flat 12 paise/₹100 to **risk-based** from April 1, 2026: Category A (safest) 8 paise → D 12 paise
- Cover remains ₹5 lakh/depositor/bank

**G) Acquisition Financing & REIT Lending (February drafts)**
- Acquisition financing: bank exposure cap raised to 20% of capital (from proposed 10%); can now finance unlisted-company buyouts (target net worth ≥₹500 Cr); financing portion raised to 75% (from 70%); effective April 1, 2026
- REIT lending: bank exposure capped at 10% of eligible capital; combined bank exposure to a REIT capped at 49% of REIT value; effective July 1, 2026

**H) External Commercial Borrowings (ECB) — revised guidelines (February)**
- Cap raised to higher of **$1 billion** or 300% of net worth (from $750 million); India Inc raised record $61 bn via ECB in FY25

**I) International & Institutional MoUs (January cluster)**
- **RBI-ESMA (EU)** MoU restores EU clearing-member access to Indian CCPs, resolving an Oct 2022 dispute
- **RBI-Delhi Govt** MoU — RBI to act as Delhi's banker/debt manager for the first time, enabling market borrowing
- RBI recognised **FEDAI** (est. 1958) as a Self-Regulatory Organisation
- Revised Integrated Ombudsman Scheme effective July 1, 2026 (compensation caps ₹30 lakh/₹3 lakh)

**J) Other RBI actions this two-month span**
- Lead Bank Scheme (1969) draft: rural/semi-urban CD ratio mandated at 60%
- RBI's Responsible Business Conduct (Amendment) Directions, 2026 (draft) — first formal definition of mis-selling; effective July 1, 2026
- UTI mandatory for OTC derivative trades from Jan 1, 2027
- 5 financial institutions penalised ₹1.35 Cr total in February
- RBI's 2nd data centre built in Bhubaneswar, Odisha (Tier IV certified)
- RBI's Digital Payments Index (DPI) rose to 516.76 (Sep 2025), from 465.33 (Sep 2024)
- Gold purchases fell 94% to 4.02 tonnes in 2025 (8-year low), even as total reserves hit a record 880.2 tonnes

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — Repo rate 5.25% (confirmed held through Aug 2026), the dividend payout cap's final 75%/80% split, MSME collateral-free loan ₹10L→₹20L, ECB cap $750mn→$1bn, and "first-ever 90-day VRR" are the five standing-exception, always-ask items from this whole two-month sweep.

💼 Interview Q → *"Why did RBI need its first-ever 90-day repo in January 2026?"* — Advance-tax and GST outflows created an unusually large systemic cash crunch in late January; RBI's previous longest VRR tenor was 56 days, so extending to 90 days (alongside a $10bn FX swap and OMO purchases) reflected the scale of the liquidity gap it needed to bridge before the repo rate's own February review.
