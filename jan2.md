  - 🚫 Also projected by OECD, S&P, ADB, Fitch — similar range, not repeating individually

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — "India = 4th-largest economy, $4.18 trillion, overtook Japan" is close to certain to appear; don't confuse with the separate 2030 target of overtaking Germany for 3rd place.

## 🏛️ REGULATORY BODIES NEWS (RBI / SEBI / IRDAI)

### 📰 RBI Governance & Prudential Norms — Amendment Round-up *(merged: NBFC capital adequacy, bank dividend cap, credit risk/RPT, PSL directions, UCB director cooling-off)*

- **NBFC Prudential Norms Amendment, 2026**: Loans to high-quality infra projects get 75% risk-weight if borrower repaid ≥2% of debt (draft had proposed ≥5–10%); 50% risk-weight if repaid ≥5% (draft: ≥10%) — effective **Apr 1, 2026**
- **Bank Dividend Payout**: RBI proposed raising the cap from **40% to 75%** of PAT, with a graded 10-bucket structure tied to CET1 capital; effective FY27; excludes SFBs/payments banks/RRBs/LABs
- **Credit Risk Management (Related Party Transactions)**: widened definition of "related parties" (promoters, directors, KMPs, >10% shareholders); new materiality thresholds by bank asset size (₹50 Cr for banks >₹10 lakh Cr assets, down to ₹5 Cr for banks <₹1 lakh Cr); effective Apr 1, 2026
- **PSL Targets & Classification Directions, 2025 amendments**: SFB target corrected to **60%** of ANBC (was mistakenly 75%); PSLC framework codified into 4 types; banks can issue PSLCs up to 50% of prior year's PSL achievement
- **UCB/Cooperative Bank director cooling-off**: proposed 3-year cooling-off after 10 years of continuous board service (comments invited till Jan 30, 2026)

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — The dividend payout cap change (40%→75%) is the single most quotable RBI number this month.

### 📰 SEBI Regulatory Changes *(merged: Mutual Funds Regulations 2026, Merchant Bankers norms, AIF compliance officer certification)*
