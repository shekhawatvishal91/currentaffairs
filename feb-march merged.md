🪝 Hook — The first Budget drafted in Kartavya Bhawan cuts income-tax rules from 819 to 536 sections and unveils a ₹10,000 crore SME Growth Fund.

- TCS cuts: Overseas tour packages → 2% (from 5%/20%); LRS education/medical → 2% (from 5%)

📰 **RBI (Responsible Business Conduct) Third Amendment Directions, 2026 — Full Lifecycle** *(Feb draft origin → March draft detail → confirmed final status, merged as single regulatory instrument)*

🪝 Hook — One RBI rulebook, three sightings across two months: first as a mis-selling reform, then as a digital-fraud compensation draft, and now — confirmed via live search — a finalised rule with a further-delayed rollout.

- **Feb draft**: formally defines "mis-selling" for the first time; restricts sales calls to 9 AM–6 PM; feedback due Mar 4, 2026; proposed effective **July 1, 2026**
- **March draft detail**: victims of small-value digital banking fraud (loss up to ₹50,000) eligible for compensation up to **85% of net loss or ₹25,000, whichever is lower**; cost-sharing varies by loss band (e.g., for losses <₹29,412: RBI bears 65%, customer's bank 10%, beneficiary bank 10% of the 85% compensation paid)
- 🔄 **Confirmed final status (live-verified)**: Directions formally issued **June 24, 2026**. RBI **further delayed** the effective date to **January 1, 2027** (from the proposed July 1, 2026), giving banks more time to resolve complaints; some consumer-protection provisions were modified from the draft. Zero customer liability applies where fraud is due to bank negligence or promptly-reported third-party breaches; compensation window in force for 1 year from the effective date.
- Note: this replaces an earlier, less-precise description of a "victim+bank 15% each" compensation split that appeared in some Feb-cycle reporting — the accurate, confirmed mechanic is the 85%/₹25,000 formula above.

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — 85% of loss / ₹25,000 cap is the headline number; the trap is the **effective date**, which moved twice (draft: July 1, 2026 → final: January 1, 2027) — know the final date, not the draft one.
