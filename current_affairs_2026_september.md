📰 **IFSCA Notifies Market Abuse Regulations 2026 in GIFT IFSC: Replaces SEBI PIT and PFUTP Regimes**

- **Genesis & Regulatory Autonomy**: The International Financial Services Centres Authority (**IFSCA**) notified the *IFSCA (Market Abuse) Regulations, 2026*, establishing a dedicated statutory framework to prevent insider trading, market manipulation, and unlawful disclosure of inside information in GIFT City.
- **Dismantling Domestic SEBI Regime**:
  - From the date of commencement, domestic SEBI regulations—namely the *SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading / PIT) Regulations, 2015* and *SEBI (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices / PFUTP) Regulations, 2003*—**will no longer apply within IFSC**.
  - IFSCA assumes exclusive regulatory and enforcement jurisdiction over all securities listed or traded on IFSC exchanges.
- **Mandatory Trade Disclosure Threshold**:
  - Designated persons and insiders must disclose quarterly trades exceeding **\$25,000 within 2 business days** to the compliance officer and the exchange.
- **Statutory Enforcement Powers**:
  - Empowers IFSCA to issue formal warnings, censures, monetary penalties, disgorgement of illegal profits, and suspension or cancellation of registration of intermediaries and listed entities under Section 12 & Section 13 of the *IFSCA Act, 2019*.

🎯 Exam Angle →
- Regulatory Replacement: Replaces SEBI PIT 2015 and SEBI PFUTP 2003 in IFSC.
- Quarterly Disclosure Threshold: Trades above **\$25,000 within 2 business days** (crucial numerical question).
- Governing Law: **IFSCA Act, 2019** (HQ: GIFT City, Gandhinagar; Chairperson: K. Rajaraman).
- Target MCQ Form: "Under the newly notified IFSCA Market Abuse Regulations 2026, what is the trade disclosure threshold for designated persons?" → Trades exceeding \$25,000 within 2 business days.

📰 **PFRDA Mandates 5 Equity-Based Risk Categories for NPS Multiple Scheme Framework (MSF)**

- **Standardised Risk Classification**: The Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (**PFRDA**) introduced a uniform 5-tier classification system for all schemes under the **Multiple Scheme Framework (MSF)** to simplify subscriber comparison.
- **The 5 Mandated Equity Categories**:
  - **Category A**: **80%–100% Equity allocation** → *Aggressive Growth / Very High Risk*
  - **Category B**: **60%–80% Equity allocation** → *High Growth / High Risk*
  - **Category C**: **35%–60% Equity allocation** → *Balanced Growth / Medium Risk*
  - **Category D**: **10%–35% Equity allocation** → *Conservative Growth / Low to Medium Risk*
  - **Category E**: **0%–10% Equity allocation** → *Debt-oriented / Low Risk*
- **Portfolio Restructuring**: All existing Pension Fund Managers (PFMs) are mandated to restructure and rename their existing MSF offerings to strictly adhere to these prescribed equity bands and risk nomenclatures.

- **The 100-Point Scoring Model Across 9 Parameters**:
  - **System Availability**: **20% weightage** (core transaction processing uptime, failover efficiency, and near-zero RPO/RTO).
  - **Cybersecurity Posture**: **20% weightage** (threat detection capabilities, SOC defense, and perimeter vulnerability mitigation).
  - **Data Integrity**: **10% weightage** (ledger consistency, transaction reconciliation, and tamper-resistance).
  - **IT Governance**: **10% weightage** (board-level technology oversight, risk frameworks, and regulatory reporting).
  - **Reliability & Performance Monitoring**: **10% weightage** (latency benchmarks, throughput stress testing, and real-time observability).
  - **Business Continuity Planning (BCP) & Disaster Recovery (DR)**: **10% weightage** (live DR drills and secondary data centre operational parity).
  - **Modularity & Architecture Flexibility**: **10% weightage** (component decoupling, API resilience, and scalable middleware).
  - **System Scalability**: **5% weightage** (peak trading volume headroom and capacity planning).
  - **Incident Handling & Root Cause Analysis**: **5% weightage** (mean time to detect/resolve and statutory forensic disclosures).
- **Implementation Mechanism**: The **Industry Standards Forum (ISF)**, comprising technical leaders from MIIs, has been mandated to formulate standardized assessment metrics, automated data feeds, and quarterly self-assessment scorecards.

🎯 Exam Angle →
