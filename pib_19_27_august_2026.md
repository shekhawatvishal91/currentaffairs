📰 **Index of Core Industries (ICI) — New Series with Base Year 2022–23 & 9 Core Industries** *(PIB 20 Aug)*

🪝 The benchmark Index of Core Industries has expanded from 8 to 9 sectors, adopting the 2022–23 base year and introducing Iron Ore into the core basket.

- **Growth Snapshot**: ICI grew **5.4%** in July 2026 (Provisional) YoY, compared to **6.0%** in June 2026 (Final Index).
- **Released by**: Office of Economic Adviser (OEA), Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce & Industry. Release schedule: 20th of the following month (or next working day).
- **Structural Overhaul**:
  - **Base Year**: Shifted from **2011–12 → 2022–23**.
  - **Basket Expansion**: Increased from **8 to 9 industries** with the inclusion of **Iron Ore**.
  - **Weightage Distribution (Descending Order)**:
    1. **Electricity**: **30.932%** (Heaviest component)
    2. **Refinery Products**: **22.572%**
    3. **Steel**: **17.584%**
    4. **Crude Oil**: **7.430%**
    5. **Coal**: **5.596%**
    6. **Iron Ore**: **4.905%** *(Newly added 9th industry)*
    7. **Cement**: **4.410%**
    8. **Natural Gas**: **3.841%**
    9. **Fertilizers**: **2.731%** (Smallest component)
- **Key Methodological Changes**:
  - **Steel**: Measured using **Gross Production** instead of Net Production, harmonizing with the Index of Industrial Production (IIP).
  - **Coal**: Only **Raw Coal** is retained; Coal Middling and Washed Coal excluded to avoid double counting.
  - **Linking Factor**: Overall linking factor between old (2011–12) and new (2022–23) series = **1.47**.

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — Don't confuse the new 9th core industry (**Iron Ore**, 4.905%) with existing minerals; memorize the top 3 weights: Electricity (30.932%) > Refinery (22.572%) > Steel (17.584%); linking factor is **1.47**.

## 2. 🏛️ REGULATORY, SCHEMES & CENTRAL POLICY NOTIFICATIONS

📰 **Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (MPMS) — Official Notification by MeitY** *(PIB 21–22 Aug)*

🪝 MeitY officially notified the operational guidelines for the ₹62,500 crore MPMS, laying down stringent eligibility tiers for global manufacturers vs Indian-owned brands.

- **Outlay & Tenure**: **₹62,500 crore** over 5 years (**FY 2026–27 to FY 2030–31**); succeeds PLI-LSEM (which expired 31 March 2026).
- **Target Segments & Two-Tier Incentive Matrix**:
  - **Target Segment 1 (TS1) — General Mobile Phone Manufacturing (Global & Domestic)**:
    - **Incentive**: **2.25% to 5.0%** on incremental sales.
    - **Eligibility**: Minimum turnover of **₹10,000 crore** in FY 2025–26; existing brands must achieve an annual incremental sales threshold of **₹5,000 crore** over FY26 base; new entrants need ₹10,000 cr initial annual sales + ₹5,000 cr incremental sales.
  - **Target Segment 2 (TS2) — Indian-Owned Mobile Phone Brands**:
    - **Incentive**: Flat **5% base incentive** + additional **3% for Indian Design & R&D** (Total up to **8%**).
    - **Eligibility**: Minimum turnover of **₹1,000 crore** in FY 2025–26; >51% Indian citizen shareholding and management control; registered IP/trademarks in India; in-house domestic R&D facilities; eligible for a **1-year gestation period**.
  - **Component Localisation Bonus (Both TS1 & TS2)**: Additional **up to 1.5%** for domestic sourcing of key components/sub-assemblies, provided localisation covers at least **25%** of total mobile phone units produced in the FY.
- **Strategic Targets**: Cumulative mobile output of **~₹39 lakh crore**; ~60,000 direct jobs; India currently makes **99.2%** of domestically consumed mobile phones (2nd-largest global producer by volume).

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — Trap: TS1 eligibility turnover (₹10,000 cr) vs TS2 Indian-brand turnover (₹1,000 cr); TS2 design/R&D bonus is 3%; component localisation bonus is 1.5% (tied to 25% minimum unit localization).

📰 **Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan (PM-AASHA) — Revamped Architecture** *(PIB 21–22 Aug)*

🪝 An integrated umbrella scheme to protect farmgate prices through 4 distinct mechanisms with a ₹7,200 crore budget allocation for FY 2026–27.

- **Launch & Mandate**: September 2018; comprehensive MSP assurance framework.
- **Four Distinct Components**:
  1. **Price Support Scheme (PSS)**: Physical procurement of pulses, oilseeds, and copra at MSP through NAFED and NCCF. Procurement limit: Initially up to **25% of State/UT production** (can be raised up to 25% of national production). *Exception*: For **Tur, Urad, and Masur**, procurement is permitted up to **100% of State production**.
  2. **Price Deficiency Payment Scheme (PDPS)**: Direct cash transfer of price difference between MSP and market price (up to **15% of MSP value**); no physical procurement; primarily applied to oilseeds.
  3. **Price Stabilization Fund (PSF)**: Maintained by Department of Consumer Affairs for strategic buffer stocking of essential food items (pulses, onion, potato) to curb consumer retail spikes; now formally integrated under PM-AASHA.
  4. **Market Intervention Scheme (MIS)**: Applied to perishable horticultural commodities (e.g., Tomato, Onion, Potato - TOP) lacking formal MSP; activated when market price crashes by **≥10%** below previous normal season rate.
- **Budget Allocation (2026–27)**: **₹7,200 crore**.

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — Don't confuse PDPS (max 15% price difference, zero procurement) with PSS (physical procurement up to 25%, but 100% for Tur/Urad/Masur); MIS trigger threshold is a 10% price fall.

📰 **National Animal Disease Control Programme (NADCP) & Dairy Productivity Milestones** *(PIB 21–22 Aug)*

- **Core Mandate**: Launched September 2019 under Livestock Health and Disease Control Programme (LHDCP, Budget FY27: **₹2,010 crore**); 100% centrally funded to control/eradicate Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD) and Brucellosis by 2030.
- **Vaccination Targets & Protocol**:
  - **FMD**: 100% vaccination of susceptible cattle, buffaloes, sheep, goats, and pigs **twice a year (every 6 months)**. Cumulative: **147.16 crore doses**.
  - **Brucellosis**: **One-time lifetime vaccination** for female bovine calves aged **4–8 months**.
  - **Vaccinator Remuneration**: Minimum **₹3 per vaccination dose**.
- **Digital Infrastructure**: **Bharat Pashudhan Portal** — assigns a unique **12-digit Tag ID** to every bovine; **>39 crore** livestock registered by July 2026. Doorstep delivery via **4,019 Mobile Veterinary Units (MVUs)**.
- **Epidemiological Impact**: FMD outbreaks reduced to 40 (2025); zero outbreaks of Serotype Asia-1 in 3 years; Brucellosis outbreaks dropped to 15 (2025).
- **Macro Dairy Metrics**:
  - India's annual milk production: **247.87 million tonnes** (FY 2024–25), up **69.4%** in a decade.
  - Bovine productivity: **2,250 kg/animal/year** (up 36.63%); Target: **3,000 kg/animal/year by 2030**.
- **Multilateral Linkage**: **US$25 million** Pandemic Fund project supported by G20, implemented with ADB, FAO, and World Bank.

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — FMD vaccination interval (every 6 months) vs Brucellosis (one-time lifetime at 4–8 months female calves); unique 12-digit Tag ID on Bharat Pashudhan; 3,000 kg/animal bovine productivity target by 2030.

## 4. 🌐 NATIONAL, SECTORAL & COMMODITY SPECIALS

📰 **India's Makhana Sector — Traditional Crop to Global Superfood** *(PIB 19 Aug Backgrounder)*

🪝 Bihar accounts for 80–85% of global makhana supply, backed by a dedicated Central Sector Scheme and National Board.

- **Botanical Identity**: Makhana (*Euryale ferox*), also known as fox nut or gorgon nut, aquatic crop grown in wetlands.
- **Geographical Concentration**: India is the world's leading producer; **Bihar produces ~75% of India's output and 80–85% of global supply** (Kosi basin: Supaul, Saharsa, Madhepura, Mithila, Seemanchal). 'Mithila Makhana' holds a registered **GI Tag**.
- **Institutional & Scheme Backing**:
  - **National Makhana Board**: Announced in Union Budget 2025–26, launched in Bihar on **15 September 2025**.
  - **Central Sector Scheme for Development of Makhana**: Total outlay **₹476.03 crore** (FY 2025–26 to 2030–31); Allocations: ₹30 cr (FY26), ₹90 cr (FY27).
- **Production & Market Stats**:
  - National Production (2025–26 2nd AE): **80,590 MT** (productivity 2.34 MT/ha); Bihar accounts for 60,000 MT. Improved varieties: *Swarna Vaidehi* and *Sabour Makhana-1*.
  - Market Size: Domestic market projected to reach **₹11,000–12,000 crore by 2029–30** (17–18% CAGR).
  - Export Geography: India exports ~40% of production (~0.25 lakh MT). In 2025–26, exports stood at 7,264.89 MT (₹192.96 crore). **USA (40%) > Canada > UAE** account for **77%** of exports.

🎯 Exam Angle → ₹476.03 crore Central Sector Scheme outlay; Bihar's 80–85% global dominance; USA as top export destination (40%).

📰 **National Report on Deep-Sea and Distant-Water Fishery Resources of Indian EEZ** *(PIB 20 Aug)*

- **Released by**: Centre for Marine Living Resources & Ecology (**CMLRE**), Kochi, under Ministry of Earth Sciences.
- **Survey Base**: Synthesizes 40 years of oceanographic research and 30 years of systematic cruise surveys by India's flagship Fishery and Oceanographic Research Vessel (**FORV) Sagar Sampada**.
- **Key Discovery — Mesopelagic "Twilight Zone"**: Mapped resources between **200 to 1,000 metres depth**, highlighting deep-sea lanternfishes (Myctophids) that drive the oceanic biological carbon pump.
- **Identified Biodiversity Hotspots**: Kollam Bank, Angria Bank, Off-Mangalore Deep-Sea Slope, Terrace off Trivandrum, Lakshadweep, and Andaman & Nicobar EEZ (covering India's **2.37 million sq. km EEZ**).

🎯 Exam Angle → FORV Sagar Sampada as flagship vessel; Twilight zone (200–1,000 m); Kollam and Angria banks as deep-sea hotspots.

📰 **Regenerative Agriculture & National Mission on Natural Farming (NMNF)** *(PIB 23–24 Aug Backgrounder)*

- **Agricultural Growth Context**: India achieved **4.45% decadal growth** in agriculture (FY16–FY25), the highest in recorded history. Linked to **SDG 13.1** (Climate Action).
- **National Mission on Natural Farming (NMNF)**:
  - Launched **2024**; provides an output-based incentive of **₹4,000 per acre per year for 2 years** (up to 1 acre/farmer).
  - Establishes **10,000 Bio-input Resource Centres** for local formulations (*Jeevamrit*, *Beejamrit*).
  - Cluster Model: 18,786 clusters formed; each cluster supported by **2 Community Resource Persons (CRPs)**.
- **Other Sustainable Agri Interventions**:
  - **PKVY**: ₹31,500/ha over 3 years for organic conversion (16.90 lakh ha covered).
  - **Per Drop More Crop (PDMC)**: 55% subsidy for small/marginal farmers (45% for others); ₹26,325 crore released.
  - **Rainfed Area Development (RAD)**: ₹30,000/family for Integrated Farming Systems (IFS) + ₹10,000/cluster training.
  - **Soil Health Card Scheme**: Launched 2015; tests **12 parameters** every 2 years; **25.89 crore cards generated**.

🎯 Exam Angle → NMNF's ₹4,000/acre/year incentive for 2 years; 10,000 Bio-input Resource Centres; Soil Health Card's 12 parameters.

📰 **India's Sugar & Sugarcane Industry Snapshot** *(PIB 25–26 Aug)*

- **Global Standing**: India is the **2nd-largest sugarcane producer** globally, supporting ~5 crore farmers and ~5 lakh mill workers.
- **Production (2025–26, 3rd AE)**: Sugarcane output surged **~43.5% to 500 MMT** across 58.87 lakh ha. Top producers: **Uttar Pradesh & Maharashtra**.
- **Sugar Dynamics**: Annual sugar production 300–340 lakh MT; domestic consumption 280–290 lakh MT; exports 8 lakh MT (key markets: Sri Lanka, West Asia, East Africa).

- **Supply Controls**: 400-tonne dealer stock limit (1 Aug–30 Nov 2026); bulk consumers barred from holding stocks exceeding **15 days of consumption** from 1 Sept 2026; 10 LMT duty-free raw sugar import quota.

🎯 Exam Angle → FRP of ₹365/qtl at 10.25% recovery; 75% ethanol feedstock now coming from grains/maize.
