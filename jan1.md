### 📰 Census 2027 — Phase Structure & Caste Enumeration *(merged: items 26 + 73)*

🪝 Hook — For the first time since 1931, India's next Census will count caste for every community — and it's also going fully digital.

- 1st phase (**Houselisting Operations, HLO**) – **Apr 1, 2026 to Sep 30, 2026**; 33 questions on housing/assets; self-enumeration option available 15 days before HLO starts
- 2nd phase (**Population Enumeration, PE**) – **Feb 2027**; individual demographic data incl. **caste** collected here — 1st caste enumeration since 1931, covering all communities (not just SC/ST)
- Census 2027 = **16th** official Census, **8th** since Independence | Reference date: 12 a.m., **Mar 1, 2027** (Ladakh & snowbound J&K/HP/Uttarakhand areas: **Oct 1, 2026**)
- Governed by **Census Act, 1948**; Census is Entry 69, Union List, 7th Schedule, Article 246 | Conducted by RG&CCI (MHA)
- First Census after 2026 count can be used for Lok Sabha delimitation & 33% women's reservation rollout

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — Phase dates + "1st digital census, 1st caste count since 1931" is a near-certain MCQ; don't confuse HLO (Apr–Sep 2026) with PE (Feb 2027, where caste is counted).

### 📰 UN High Seas Treaty (BBNJ) Enters Into Force

### 📰 Coking Coal Declared 'Critical and Strategic Mineral'

- Notified under **MMDR Act, 1957**, based on HLC-VB recommendations + NITI Aayog inputs
- India has ~37.37 billion tonnes of coking coal resources (mainly Jharkhand); yet ~95% of steel-sector requirement is imported (mainly Australia, Russia, USA)
- Imports rose from 51.20 MT (2020-21) to 57.58 MT (2024-25)
- New status enables faster environmental clearance & degraded-forest-land use for afforestation

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — Despite 37.37 bn tonnes domestic resource, ~95% import dependency is the sharp contrast worth flagging.
