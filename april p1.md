📰 **CBDT — Income-tax Rules, 2026 & "Kar Saathi"** *(merged: new rules + AI assistant launch)*

🪝 Hook — CBDT slimmed 511 tax rules down to 333 and rolled out an AI chatbot, "Kar Saathi," to help taxpayers navigate the new Income Tax Act, 2025 regime — effective April 1, 2026.

- **Tax Year** replaces 'Previous Year'/'Assessment Year'

- **Reliefs**: children's education allowance ₹100 → **₹3,000/month** (max 2 children); hostel allowance → ₹9,000; employer gifts exempt up to **₹15,000**; meal exemption → ₹200/meal
- **HRA exemption expanded**: to Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru (50% slab, previously only 4 metros)
- **PAN mandatory for**: property deals only above **₹20 lakh** (up from ₹10 lakh)
- **Kar Saathi** — AI-enabled 24x7 taxpayer assistant, part of **'PRARAMBH 2026'** (Policy Reform and Responsible Action for Mission Viksit Bharat) campaign
- **CBDT**: HQ New Delhi | Chairman Ravi Agrawal | Formed 1944

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — "Tax Year" terminology + PRARAMBH 2026 full form + effective date (April 1, 2026).

💼 Interview Q → *Why replace "Previous Year/Assessment Year" with a single "Tax Year"?* → Simplifies compliance under the new Income Tax Act, 2025 by removing the dual-year confusion that often causes filing errors.

📰 **RBI — Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Mandatory for Digital Payments**

🪝 Hook — A single OTP will no longer cut it: from April 1, 2026, every UPI, card, and wallet transaction needs a second layer of verification.

- **2FA mandatory for**: UPI, debit/credit cards, mobile wallets — OTP alone insufficient
- **Second layer**: PIN, password, biometric, or facial recognition
- **Banks/platforms may be**: liable to compensate customers for fraud due to system failure
- **Full implementation (incl.**: cross-border card payments) by **October 2026**

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — Effective date (Apr 1, 2026) vs full implementation date (Oct 2026) — classic date-confusion trap.

📰 **RBI — Digital Payments Fraud Safeguards (Discussion Paper)**

🪝 Hook — RBI's proposed fix for UPI fraud: force a one-hour "cooling-off" pause on big transfers, borrowing straight from the "golden hour" principle in crime prevention.

- **1-hour cooling-off period** proposed for A2A transfers above ₹10,000 (no chargeback option currently exists)
- **Threshold chosen because**: ₹10,000+ transfers = ~45% of fraud cases by volume, ~98.5% by value
- **Transactions above ₹50,000**: → additional verification (trusted-individual approval)
- **Mule account** annual credit cap proposed at **₹25 lakh**
- **New user controls**: channel on/off toggle, custom limits, emergency "kill switch"

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — ₹10,000 (cooling-off threshold) vs ₹50,000 (extra verification) vs ₹25 lakh (mule account cap) — three-number trap cluster.

💼 Interview Q → *What is the "golden hour" principle referenced by RBI?* → The idea that the period immediately after a fraudulent transaction is most critical to prevent dissipation of funds — hence the proposed 1-hour delay window.

📰 **RBI — NBFC Regulatory Overhaul Cluster** *(merged: 3-layer proposal, NBFC-UL threshold change, branch authorisation, TReDS)*

🪝 Hook — RBI is rewriting the NBFC rulebook on three fronts at once: how NBFCs are layered, how the "upper layer" is defined, and how easily they can open branches.

- **A.** Proposed new **3-layer** framework (Upper/Middle/Lower) to replace existing 4-layer Scale-Based Regulation (Base/Middle/Upper/Top, Oct 2021)
- **B.** Proposed replacing NBFC-UL identification methodology with a flat **₹1 lakh crore asset threshold** (vs. current Top-10 + parametric scoring: 70% quantitative, 30% qualitative); to be reviewed every 5 years. Also proposed removing the carve-out that shielded govt-owned NBFCs (NABARD, Exim Bank, SIDBI) from UL classification — aligning to "ownership-neutral regulation"
- **C.** NBFC Branch Authorisation, 2026 — NBFCs can open branches without prior RBI approval based on Net Owned Funds (NOF) & credit rating thresholds (₹50 crore NOF / AA rating benchmark)
- **D.** Proposed dropping due-diligence requirement for MSMEs onboarding **TReDS** (Trade Receivables Discounting System)

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — ₹1 lakh crore NBFC-UL threshold is the standout figure across this cluster.

⏰ Status flag → All four are draft/proposed as of April 2026 — check for finalisation before exam.

📰 **RBI — Paytm Payments Bank Licence Cancelled**

🪝 Hook — Four years after being barred from onboarding new customers, Paytm Payments Bank's banking licence was formally cancelled in April 2026.

- **Licence cancelled under**: **Section 22(4), Banking Regulation Act, 1949**, effective close of business **April 24, 2026**
- **Grounds**: violation of Section 22(3)(g) [licence conditions] and Section 22(3)(c) [management prejudicial to depositor interest]
- **RBI to apply**: for winding up before High Court; bank has enough liquidity to repay deposits
- **Earlier restrictions**: no new onboarding from March 11, 2022; further curbs Jan/Feb 2024

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — Section 22(4) cancellation date (April 24, 2026) vs earlier March 2022 onboarding ban — sequencing trap.

## 9. 📋 PIB, CIRCULARS & NOTIFICATIONS

📰 **Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Bill, 2026**

🪝 Hook — Parliament just decriminalised over 700 provisions across 79 laws, shifting India from a "punish first" to a "trust first" regulatory culture.

- **Amends **784 provisions****: across **79 Central Acts**, 23 Ministries
- **717 decriminalised** (Ease of Doing Business) + **67 amended** (Ease of Living)
- **Touches RBI Act**: 1934, Food Safety & Standards Act 2006, Motor Vehicles Act 1988

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — 784 total / 717 decriminalised / 67 ease-of-living split is a precise, testable number set.

---

📰 **Central Armed Police Forces (General Administration) Act, 2026**

- **Mandates IPS deputation quotas in CAPF**: 50% IG posts, 67% ADG posts, all SDG/DG posts
- **Overrides a May**: 2025 SC judgment that sought to progressively reduce IPS deputation in CAPFs
- **CAPF = Assam**: Rifles, BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, NSG, SSB (7 forces, MHA)

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — 50%/67%/100% IPS deputation quotas by rank is a classic number-matching MCQ.

📰 **Solid Waste Management (SWM) Rules, 2026**

- **Replaces 2016 rules,**: effective April 1, 2026, under Environment (Protection) Act, 1986
- **4-stream colour-coded segregation**: Green (wet), Blue (dry), Red (sanitary), Black (special/hazardous)
- **Bulk Waste Generator threshold**: 100 kg/day waste, 20,000 sqm floor area, or 40,000 litres/day water use

🎯 Exam Angle → Colour-code matching (Green/Blue/Red/Black → waste type) is the standout MCQ format.
