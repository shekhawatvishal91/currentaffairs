📰 **India's 100 GW Nuclear Power Roadmap by 2047**

🪝 Hook — India wants to go from 8.8 GW to 100 GW of nuclear power in 21 years — but first it needs to cut approval timelines almost in half.

- **Target**: **100 GW by 2047** (from current 8.8 GW) — over 10x scale-up
- **Enabling law**: **SHANTI Act** (Sustainable Harnessing and Advancement of Nuclear Energy for Transforming India)
- **Approval timeline to be cut from **13 years to 8**– 9 years**
- **Investment needed**: ≥₹20 lakh crore (at ₹22 crore/MW baseline)

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — 8.8 GW → 100 GW target and the SHANTI Act name are the two hooks most likely tested.

## 6. 🔬 SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY, DEFENCE & SPORTS

📰 **NASA's Artemis II Mission**

🪝 Hook — Humans are heading back toward the Moon for the first time since 1972 — no landing yet, just history's farthest-ever human spaceflight.

- **Launched **April 1,**: 2026**; 1st crewed Artemis mission; 1st human Moon-vicinity trip since Apollo 17 (1972)
- **Crew**: Reid Wiseman (commander), Victor Glover (1st person of colour beyond LEO), Christina Koch (1st woman to the Moon's vicinity), Jeremy Hansen (1st Canadian/non-American beyond Earth orbit)
- **10-day lunar flyby**: (free-return trajectory); travels ~4,06,000 km — farthest humans have ever travelled (surpassing Apollo 13's ~4,00,000 km/1970 record)
- **Tests Laser Communications**: (O2O) at 260 Mbps; **Artemis IV (2028)** targets first crewed South Pole landing

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — Match astronaut to "first" superlative (Glover/Koch/Hansen); farthest-distance record fact is a strong trap vs Apollo 13.
