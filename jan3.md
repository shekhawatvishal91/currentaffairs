### 📰 India–EU Free Trade Agreement — Negotiations Concluded (Jan 27, 2026)

🪝 Hook — After 18 years of on-and-off talks, India and the EU finally struck what's being called the "Mother of All Deals" — a free-trade zone covering 2 billion people and a quarter of global GDP.

- Announced at the **16th India-EU Summit, New Delhi**, by PM Modi, Ursula von der Leyen, and António Costa
- Negotiations began **2007**; concluded after **18 years**
- Creates a free-trade zone: 2 billion people, ~25% of global GDP, one-third of global trade
- India to reduce/eliminate tariffs on **96.6%** of EU goods; EU to grant preferential access for **99%+** of Indian exports by value
- Commitments in 144 services subsectors (India) / 102 (EU); also includes a Security & Defence Partnership and a Green Hydrogen Task Force
- (Cross-reference: banking provisions of this deal — 15 EU bank branches over 4 years, 100% FDI in insurance — covered in Bucket 2)

🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — "18 years of negotiation, concluded Jan 27, 2026" and the 96.6%/99% tariff figures are near-certain; the "Mother of All Deals" nickname itself is a likely direct-recall trap.
